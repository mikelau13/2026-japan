## Event Overview

**Zipangu** is the first major Japanese music festival on US soil, hosted at **Brookside at the Rose Bowl**, Pasadena.

| Doors | 3:30 PM |

## Suggested Itinerary

### Day 1 — Friday, May 15: Arrive in LA

- Fly into **LAX** — Friday flights are cheapest; arrive ~7 PM
- Clear baggage ~8 PM → rideshare to **Hollywood hotel** (~30 min) → arrive ~8:30 PM
- Late dinner on Hollywood Blvd or Sunset Strip (many restaurants open past 9 PM)

### Day 2 — Saturday, May 16: ZIPANGU Festival Day 🎵

| Time | Plan |
|------|------|
| Morning | Leisurely breakfast; rest up |
| ~2:00 PM | Rideshare to Rose Bowl (~30 min from Hollywood) |
| 3:30 PM | Doors open |
| ~4:00 PM | First acts begin |
| ~10:00 PM+ | Ado closes the night |
| Post-show | Rideshare back to Hollywood hotel (~30 min) |

**Getting to the Rose Bowl from Hollywood:**
- **Rideshare** (Uber/Lyft): ~30 min, most convenient from Hollywood; expect surge pricing post-show
- **Drive**: Stack parking on Brookside Golf Course — expect a long exit wait

### Day 3 — Sunday, May 17: Hollywood Sightseeing → Depart

| Time | Plan |
|------|------|
| Morning | Leisurely breakfast near hotel |
| ~9:00 AM | **Hollywood Walk of Fame** — stroll Hollywood Blvd, spot celebrity stars; early morning means fewer crowds and great photos |
| ~10:00 AM | **TCL Chinese Theatre** — iconic handprint forecourt (open from 8 AM, free to browse outside) |
| ~10:30 AM | **Griffith Observatory** — short 15 min drive up the hill; free admission, panoramic LA views, Hollywood Sign backdrop. Open from 10 AM |
| ~12:30 PM | Head back, grab lunch near hotel |
| 1:30 PM | Rideshare to LAX (~35 min) |
| 2:30 PM | Arrive LAX — check in (2 hrs before international departure) |
| 4:30 PM | ✈️ Flight back to Toronto (YYZ) |

> **Tip**: Griffith Observatory can get crowded by midday — go right at 10 AM opening for the best experience.

## Practical Notes

- **Weather**: Hollywood in mid-May averages 72–80°F (22–27°C) daytime, cooler at night (55–62°F / 13–17°C). Bring a light layer for the evening festival sets.
- **Festival essentials**: sunscreen, portable charger, cash + cards, comfortable shoes
- **Language**: English primary
- **Time zone**: Pacific Time (PT) — 16 hours behind JST
